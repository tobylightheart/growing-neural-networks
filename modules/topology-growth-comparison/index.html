Mechanism comparison

Do not call every added node “more hidden capacity”

GNG and GWR grow prototypes and graph topology in input space. DNC and Cascade-Correlation add hidden features inside a supervised predictor. Their nodes, signals, and consequences are different.

Not human-reviewed. Automated source-grounded synthesis; inspect the linked limits.

GNG · scheduled growth

Accumulate where representation is poor

Which represented region has accumulated the most squared-distance error?

Prototypes move on every input. Competitive-Hebbian edges age and reset. Every λ inputs, a prototype is inserted between the largest-error node and its largest-error neighbor.

GWR · conditional growth

Grow only when this input requires it

Is the best match weak, and has its winner habituated enough?

GWR inserts immediately only when both tests pass; otherwise the winner and neighbors adapt. Growth can pause and resume without ending learning.

DNC / CasCor · hidden features

Change a supervised predictor

Has prediction learning stalled, and what new feature can reduce residual error?

DNC adds a hidden unit after normalized global error progress flattens and continues backpropagation. Cascade-Correlation installs a residual-correlated candidate and freezes its incoming weights.
