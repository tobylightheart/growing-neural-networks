Simulation Expansion and Contraction

In thesis-derived constructive spiking networks, adding a neuron can be interpreted as transferring a quiet, plausible surrounding component into the simulated set. Try expanding or contracting the simulation and watch the disruption estimate.

Rule of thumb

Low activity and sparse connectivity are better candidates for biologically plausible expansion or contraction because their transfer causes a smaller simulated activity shift.
