Growing Neural Networks

Runnable labs

Small, inspectable implementations and static experiments for constructive, growing, pruning, and plastic neural-network mechanisms.

Design principles

  • No heavy ML frameworks or build step by default.
  • Each lab runs locally and is readable in one sitting.
  • Claims stay modest unless tests and sources support more.
  • Repository-local links connect execution to literature context.
