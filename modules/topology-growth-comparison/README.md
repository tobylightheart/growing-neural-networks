# GNG, GWR, and hidden-feature growth

A linked, explicitly not-human-reviewed comparison that separates:

- GNG's periodic, accumulated-error-driven prototype insertion;
- GWR's immediate activity-plus-habituation gate; and
- supervised hidden-feature growth in DNC and Cascade-Correlation.

The core boundary is structural: GNG/GWR move and add input-space prototypes while maintaining a neighborhood graph; hidden-feature methods change the internal representation of a supervised predictor. Links lead to both topology traces, the supervised capacity-signal comparison, and source-verification limits.
