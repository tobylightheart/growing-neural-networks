What moves?

The winner and its current graph neighbors move toward each input. The trace records every before/after prototype position.

What grows?

Every fourth input, a new prototype is inserted halfway between the largest-error node and its largest-error graph neighbor; their old edge is replaced.

What ages?

Edges incident to the winner age first. The winner–runner edge is then created or reset to zero; over-age edges would be removed.
