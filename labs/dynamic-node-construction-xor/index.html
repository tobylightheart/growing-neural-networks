What is source-grounded?

Ash specifies the normalized plateau formula, topology-window guard, logistic units, one-node start in the experiments, complete connections for the new node, small random weights in [-0.1666, 0.1666], and continued ordinary backpropagation. The fixed order, deterministic LCG, one-insertion cap, default 250-sweep window, and 0.002 threshold are explicit reproduction choices.

The paper's reported defaults were a 1000-sweep window and 0.05 trigger slope. Try them here, but do not interpret one deterministic XOR trajectory as benchmark evidence.
