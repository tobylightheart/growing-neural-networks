Bundle guardrail

The active bundle has enough public draft reviews for a paper-card bridge: Lightheart2018 supplies the thesis taxonomy vocabulary, Schliebs2013 supplies eSNN lineage vocabulary, Wysoski2010 provides an application anchor, Kasabov2013 names the dynamic eSNN/deSNN detail slot, Wang2014 names the supervised adaptive-structure slot, and Roy2017 names the shared online structural-plasticity bridge. The axis chooser keeps Chapter 2 terms such as parameter calculation, local performance triggers, and pruning/merging visible as vocabulary/context only. It is still too early to present exact construction, pruning, rewiring, or adaptation triggers as verified algorithm steps for the later eSNN and structural-plasticity papers.
