Fit quality and retained width — longer bars mean lower error

Longer is better. Bar length is the negative log of the error, not the error itself, so the two sub-0.002 policies stay visible beside pruning’s 0.173 instead of collapsing into slivers. The printed number beside each bar is the error itself.

The counterweight

    Neither direction is monotonic. Keeping width two gives this XOR network room to fit; deleting back to width one bounds capacity but loses the fit. The point is to expose the choice, not award “more” or “less” a universal win.
